Google Play & GDPR Compliant

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Iconderry (com.iconderry.app)

1. Overview & Our Commitment

At Iconderry, your privacy is paramount. We believe digital creative tools should respect user privacy. We do not sell your personal data, inject third-party ad tracking, or record your private design graphics.

2. Information We Collect

Iconderry is designed to function as an offline-first, local tool:

  • Local Storage & IndexedDB: Your saved favorites, theme preferences, and canvas configurations remain locally stored on your client device.
  • Optional Authentication: If you voluntarily create an account to sync presets, we securely store your email address via Supabase Auth with encrypted storage.
  • Crash Analytics: Basic anonymous crash telemetry may be collected through Google Play Developer Console to detect bugs and optimize performance.

3. Android App Permissions (Google Play Requirements)

The Iconderry Android application requests minimal permissions strictly needed for its features:

• Storage Access (Write to Downloads): Allows the app to export SVGs and ultra-high-resolution PNG images directly to your phone's gallery or Downloads folder.

• Internet Access: Allows fetching community asset catalog updates and blog tutorials.

• Zero Invasive Access: No microphone, no camera, no contacts, no biometric data, and no GPS location are requested or accessed.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Iconderry does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. The app contains only design vectors, icons, and graphic editing utilities suitable for users of all ages.

5. User Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under GDPR and CCPA, you retain full rights over your data. You may delete your local data, favorites, and cached icons at any time through the in-app Settings menu or by clearing the app data in Android system settings.
